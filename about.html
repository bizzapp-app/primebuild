Who we are

Helping turn building plans into organised projects.

Primebuild Engineering Solutions is a construction and engineering support business based in Kiambu Town, Kenya, with a focus on modern residential projects and transparent communication.

Our focus is practical: help clients understand what needs to happen, coordinate the people and resources involved, and keep communication organised as construction progresses.

For clients living abroad, this approach is especially important. Distance can make ordinary construction decisions harder, so the service model is built around clear updates, records and agreed responsibilities.

Where specialist services are required, Primebuild can coordinate with the relevant qualified professionals as part of the agreed project scope.

Our Mission

To provide organised construction support, transparent communication and practical project coordination that helps clients make informed decisions about their building projects.

Our special focus

Supporting Kenyans abroad who are building back home.

We want the client to remain part of the project without needing to be physically present for every activity. Sending money home should be an act of trust that is met with clarity — not confusion, delays or family disagreement.

Our approach

Simple enough to understand. Structured enough to follow.

01

Understand the project

We start with the client's goals, site, drawings or available information, budget expectations and current stage.

02

Agree the scope

Clarify what Primebuild will coordinate and what remains the client's or another professional's responsibility.

03

Coordinate and communicate

Support the agreed construction activities while keeping the client informed through the agreed communication process.

04

Review and close out

Keep the client involved in important decisions and work toward project completion and handover within the agreed scope.
