Practical construction support for clients at home and abroad.

The core services

Support that covers the moving parts of a residential project.

Especially useful when the client cannot be on site every day.

Residential House Construction

Construction support for homes, from planning and budgeting discussions through coordinating agreed building activities with the relevant professionals. Suitable for new builds and projects already underway.

House Planning & Consultation

Help clients understand their house-building needs, project scope, available information and the next steps toward starting the project. Includes architectural and structural design consultation within the agreed scope.

Building Cost Estimation (BOQs)

Support informed budget decisions with cost discussions and bill of quantities style estimates where included in the agreed scope.

Project Coordination & Supervision

Help coordinate materials, labour, timelines, site activities and communication between clients and the relevant professionals. Construction supervision for small and medium projects where agreed.

Materials & Labour Coordination

Support clients with materials planning, sourcing and delivery coordination, plus labour arrangements within the agreed project scope.

◷

Project Monitoring

Track work completed, materials, labour activities, challenges and planned work for the following period.

Client Reporting

Agreed updates may include photographs, videos, work completed, expenditure information where applicable, issues and next steps — shared via WhatsApp or email.

Professional Coordination

Where specialist services are needed, coordinate communication with the relevant architect, engineer, quantity surveyor or other professional within the agreed scope.

Issue & Decision Escalation

Bring significant project questions, changes, delays or decisions to the client's attention so they can be discussed early.
