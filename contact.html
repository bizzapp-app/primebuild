Questions clients often ask

Before you contact us.

Can I manage my construction project from abroad?

Primebuild's service model is designed to support clients who cannot be on site every day. The exact responsibilities, reporting and communication process should be agreed for each project.

Will I receive photos and videos?

Where included in the agreed reporting scope, project updates can include photographs and videos showing relevant work and progress.

Can Primebuild coordinate materials and labour?

Materials and labour coordination can be included where agreed. The project scope should clearly state what Primebuild is responsible for and what remains the client's responsibility.

Can you work with my architect or engineer?

Yes, where specialist input is required, Primebuild can coordinate communication with the relevant professionals within the agreed project scope.

Do I need all my documents before contacting you?

No. For an initial enquiry, share what you know. You can provide drawings, BOQs, site information and other documents as they become available.

How are costs and payments handled?

Payment amounts, methods (e.g. M-Pesa or bank transfer) and schedules are agreed per project. Large-scale construction requiring legal permits is handled under appropriate formal arrangements — not as a simple informal agreement.
